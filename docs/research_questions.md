Primary Research Question

How can knowledge learned from existing labeled seizure datasets be transferred or adapted to Muse data when jointly labeled data is unavailable?

Initial Subquestions
- What signals are available in the labeled seizure datasets?
- What signals are available from the Muse device?
- Which signals are shared or comparable across the datasets?
- How different are the signal distributions between the datasets?
- Can transfer learning be used to train a model on labeled data and adapt it to Muse data?
- Can self-supervised learning learn useful representations from unlabeled Muse data?
- Can synthetic data augmentation or domain adaptation help bridge the gap between datasets?
